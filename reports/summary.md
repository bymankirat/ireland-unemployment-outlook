**Irish Unemployment Outlook (Python, pandas, NumPy):** built an automated pipeline on CSO open data (table MUM01, January 1998 to September 2026) that produces a statistical-release-style report and a 3-month unemployment forecast; benchmarked four models in a rolling-origin backtest over 56 unseen months and reported honestly where none beat a no-change forecast, with empirical 80% prediction intervals checked for coverage.
